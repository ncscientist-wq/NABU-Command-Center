# Community development roadmap

NABU Command Center v1.0 is complete and hardware verified, but its broader command-center vision leaves room for community extensions. Planned future modules and research areas include:

- General News / Retrocomputing News
- UV and additional weather context
- World Weather
- AIS maritime / vessel tracking
- APRS / receive-only amateur-radio telemetry
- Ocean / buoy conditions
- Energy monitoring: solar, battery, grid, and UPS
- Infrastructure monitoring: server, NAS, network, SNMP/HTTP/MQTT health
- Local environmental sensors: temperature, humidity, pressure, and air/water data
- MQTT / Home Assistant integration
- Owner-authorized local telemetry
- Richer gateway-assisted maps and trend history
- Native wireframe 3D and advanced graphics research
- Alternative Gateway platforms

The larger vision is a one-stop NABU terminal spanning weather, news, space, seismic, RF, maritime, energy, infrastructure, environment, and local telemetry.
