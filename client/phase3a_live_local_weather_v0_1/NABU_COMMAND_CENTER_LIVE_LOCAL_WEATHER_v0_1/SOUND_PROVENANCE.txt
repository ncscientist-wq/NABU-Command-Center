PHASE 2D-A04C3R2 SOUND / MUSIC STREAM PROVENANCE

API:
Installed z88dk psg.h get_psg()/set_psg(), already used by verified builds.

UI cues / SOUND:
- Existing bounded cue state machine remains on AY channel A.
- X controls SOUND and therefore UI cue policy only.
- UI note writes now preserve B/C mixer bits; this is the coexistence adjustment.
- Successful navigation/open/back/etc. cue policy is otherwise unchanged.
- Blocked dashboard or Task Manager page edges start no cue.
- Root cause of the refresh-loop symptom: refresh_transport() is synchronous;
  when refresh began during an active cue, service_sound() could not advance to
  its completion path and channel-A volume remained audible during the call.
- Correction: stop_cue() silences and resets A before refresh, before replacing
  a cue, at cue completion, and when SOUND is turned OFF. No delay, retry,
  transport edit, or B/C music change was introduced.

MUSIC STREAM:
- Product name: MUSIC STREAM.
- Native implementation: original local compact AY event stream; not Internet
  audio and unrelated to program-loading port 5816.
- B is the unused global toggle key.
- Cold boot default: OFF.
- ON resumes at the next retained event; it does not restart on module open/back.
- OFF clears the wait and silences music-owned voices without changing position.
- Channel B carries the original 16-note optimistic command-center melody.
- Channel C carries a calculated harmony voice from the same original events.
- The 16-event loop has bounded period/duration arrays and no allocation.

AY ownership:
- Channel A: existing UI cues, volume register 8.
- Channels B/C: Music Stream, volume registers 9/10.
- Mixer writes preserve the other subsystem's channel-enable state.
- A cue does not change music position, wait, loop, enabled state, or track ID.

Accounting:
- stream data: 48 bytes
- runtime state: 5 bytes
- music service: nonblocking; one bounded state step at the start of each normal
  scheduler service iteration (and during the modal ZIP input loop)

Audible quality/coexistence: NOT TESTED pending Owner MAME gate.
